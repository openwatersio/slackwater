Compute tidal datums from harmonic constants or from observed water levels with the new `@slackwater/datums` package: mean high and low waters, mean sea level, the astronomical tide bounds over a pinned 19-year epoch, and approximations of the low-water datums Canada, China, Japan and South Korea chart to. Every prediction takes the nodal corrections the constants were fitted under.
