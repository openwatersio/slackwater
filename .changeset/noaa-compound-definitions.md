NOAA's shallow-water compounds at Anchorage predict with the right phase. MLN2S2, 2NMLS6, 2MLNS6, 3MLS6 and 4MLS8 have their own definitions, so they no longer stand for 3MS2, 3MNS6, 4MS6, 2Mnu6 and 3Mnu8, which share their speeds. ML4, MKL2S2, 3ML8, MSK5 and MP1 take their members' phase constants, 3KM5 is built from K1+M2+K2, and RP1 takes psi1's argument. Refit any of these constituents fitted with an earlier engine. A caller's own definition that lists one of the five names as an alias of the line it shared now replaces two catalog entries, and the engine refuses it.
