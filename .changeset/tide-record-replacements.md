Resolve superseded tide-station IDs to accepted replacement models while preserving commercial-use permission. Saved IDs whose replacement would remove commercial-use permission are refused. Direct lookups of noncommercial records remain available; replacements carry their own license and attribution terms.

Nearby lists and map focus use the resolved station's canonical ID.
